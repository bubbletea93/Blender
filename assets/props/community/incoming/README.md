# Approved Shot 5 community asset intake

Both approved source models have been acquired:

- [Glass Cup by cczero (CC0)](https://blendswap.com/blend/30973/download)
- [Organic Ceramic Tray by Santiago Adrio](https://www.blenderkit.com/asset-gallery-detail/ec82565c-f623-4d81-a50a-60fbaf24a075/)

This intake folder contains no models because the originals are preserved
in dedicated asset directories:

- `../organic_ceramic_tray/source/organic-ceramic-tray_1K_f62ddb9e-d951-4b54-ad9a-5e9c01cb6cd5.blend`
- `../glass_cup_cczero/source/Glass Cup.blend`

The tray was acquired through the official BlenderKit add-on. The glass was
copied from the user's supplied download. Each directory contains a
`provenance.json` with author, license, source URL and SHA-256. Original
geometry, materials and packed source textures remain preserved.

The adapted tray is in `../manicure_community_props.blend`; the unbranded
glass holder is in `../glass_cup_cczero/brush_holder.blend`. Both are used in
the separate Shot 5 v004 scene. Full scene geometry validation passed 306
samples with no intersections and all props within the podium. The clean
glass finish and camera framing passed rendered still/reference checks at
f376, f400, f460 and f528. The complete 153-frame motion preview passed decode
and impact/recovery review. This is a 960 × 540, 64-sample review, not a 4K
production sequence. No further asset-download action is needed from the user.
